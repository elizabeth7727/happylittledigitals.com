Privacy Policy — HLD Pin Helper

Last updated: October 23, 2026

HLD Pin Helper is a small private tool used by one person, the owner of Happy Little Digitals, to create and schedule her own pins on her own Pinterest boards. It is not offered to the public.

What it does: it posts images, titles and descriptions that the owner has written to boards the owner controls.

What it collects: nothing from other people. It does not read, copy or store anyone else's profile, messages, followers, boards or activity. It does not sell or share data with anyone.

What it stores: the owner's Pinterest sign-in approval is kept only on the owner's own computer, in a protected file. It is never sent anywhere except to Pinterest, and it can be removed at any time by revoking access in Pinterest's settings.

Your rights: because no personal data about visitors or other users is collected, there is nothing to access or delete. If you have a question, send a message through the Happy Little Digitals shop on Etsy.
